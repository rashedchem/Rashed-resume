01 / ABOUT

Operations experience.
Research perspective.

I am a steel manufacturing professional and chemistry researcher with experience across induction furnace operations, ladle refining, continuous casting, production planning and quality assurance.

My industrial work centres on improving production performance, controlling process costs, optimising raw-material use, maintaining product quality and strengthening safe plant operations. Alongside my industry career, my academic research explores heavy-metal contamination, radionuclides, environmental monitoring and health-risk assessment.

This combination allows me to approach industrial challenges with both practical operational insight and an analytical, evidence-based mindset.
